---
title: Pump Monitor Android App
---

I'm developing an Android app to consume data from the pump monitor system. The app will provide features for both residents and admins, aiming to improve transparency and management of the water pump system.

## Resident Features
- View paid and pending payments
- See pump status in real time
- Receive maintenance announcements
- Access electricity and maintenance bills for transparency

## Admin Features
- Add extra fees for residents
- Monitor payments for each resident
- Add maintenance schedules and bills
- Add electricity bill values

More features are planned for future updates.
